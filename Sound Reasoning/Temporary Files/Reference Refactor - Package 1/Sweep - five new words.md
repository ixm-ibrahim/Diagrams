# Sweep — "invisible", "failed", "instrument", "phrasing", "machinery", "wrinkle", "grading"

The seven words joined the conditionally banned list (entries added to the Watch-list draft, no scope notes yet). Per the process, every sentence containing one is listed here with a proposed replacement, for the author's ruling case by case. Three have no instances to rule on: "instrument" appears nowhere; "wrinkle" appears only in Temporary Files process material (one use is the author's own quoted prompt — untouched); "grading" and its forms appear only in tmp-dump (his old notes, outside the sweep) — the nearby words "degrades" (PP2), "degraded" (01), and "upgraded" (04) are different words, his call whether the entry covers them. Quoted flawed material (Bad examples, quoted objections) keeps its flaws and is not listed. Where a ruled fix lands in a file that a later package redrafts, it is applied there.
