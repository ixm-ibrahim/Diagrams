# 07 Phenomenological Dictionary

Every term the nodes have introduced so far, in one place: what each means, and which node grants it — so any use of a word can be checked against the earliest node that makes it available (a word granted by PP2 cannot appear in PP1). An introduced term covers its grammatical forms, and a word's meaning shifts naturally with its form — the noun "appearance" names what the verb "appear" does. A term with one meaning is one line; a homonym becomes a parent line with one sub-bullet per meaning, each with its own node.

## Waiting words

Words the project already uses that no node grants yet, each with its candidate home — so the open ruling isn't lost. Entries move up into the dictionary when their node grants them.

- **is** (the linking "is" — the "is" that joins a thing to what it is called) — no ruling yet; first needed by the future associations node's expected wording ("associations are a type of quality").
- **nothing** — held back from PP2 by his ruling; candidates: a compound of "not" and "thing", or the future node that grounds absence.
- **with** — dropped from PP2's terms; candidate: the future associations node.
- **than**, in the degree sense ("more than") — waits for a later node; the comparing sense ("different than") is granted at [[PP2 - Distinction]].
- **separate**, in the cut-apart, spatial sense — waits for a later node; the not-like-another sense is granted at [[PP2 - Distinction]] as a synonym of "distinct".
