## What this project is

I'm building a **portable reasoning-control document** to hand an AI alongside any prompt. It governs three things at once: the *logic* the AI uses, the *language* (terms must be grounded and scope-fixed), and the *framing* (how conclusions are presented, with their limits and confidence visible). The motivating problem: AI and people routinely treat weak arguments as if they had strength — "pseudo-profound BS" — and I want an instrument that makes that impossible to slip past, in the service of objective truth (including making it easy for an opponent to expose my own errors).

**The current deliverable already exists:** `Sound Reasoning/Coherence Audit - Control Document (v1).md`. Read it first. This handoff explains the reasoning that produced it, so the *why* is recoverable, not just the *what*.

## How to work with me (important — this is the meta-goal in action)

**Demystify, or you've failed the task.** Do not use a term you haven't grounded. I will (and did, repeatedly) stop and ask "what does that mean?" when a word floats free of anything I can recognize. The fix that worked every time was **show, don't label**: a worked example or a concrete sample instead of an abstract category. When you offer me a choice, make the options recognizable by example, not by jargon. Be concise; cut words that don't change the meaning.

## The derivation, in the order we built it

This chain *is* the content. Each step was pressure-tested before the next.

1. **The floor.** Everything reduces to one rule: *truth must be distinguishable from falsehood.* Any move that, applied consistently, would justify a claim and its contradiction equally is not reasoning.

2. **The floor is self-grounding, not an arbitrary axiom.** It can't be *derived* (that regresses), but it isn't *stipulated* either: **denying it requires using it** (to assert "truth needn't be distinguishable from falsehood" is to offer that as true-rather-than-false). This is retortion / performative grounding — the shape of Aristotle's defense of non-contradiction and Descartes' cogito. The regress ends here because this is the one point you can't occupy the act of arguing without already standing on.

3. **Two kinds of "grounding," kept distinct.** *Derived* = justified from prior claims (regresses). *Recognized* = seen directly in experience, not derived but not invented (my DAG primitives, e.g. "Phenomena occur"). "Precedent" in my usage means the recognized foundation a claim rests on, terminating in direct experience — **not** an endless chicken-and-egg.

4. **The mechanism under the floor is coherence.** Falsehood = incoherence = a failure of *conceptual fit*. "Coherence" must mean nothing grander than **two named things fitting without a clash** — and every use must name its two relata, or it equivocates (which the floor forbids).

5. **The "space" between the floor and the checks is the question: *how can truth be confused with falsehood?*** The checks are the complete list of answers. We derived them rather than asserting them: each is a *necessary condition for a claim to be distinguishable from its negation*, and each corresponds to a different pair of things that must cohere.

6. **The seams (the checks).** A claim can come apart in five places, plus a sixth in argument-mode:
   - **Seam 1 — word ↔ world:** terms refer to something recognizable.
   - **Seam 2 — word ↔ word:** the terms' scopes fit internally (no "square circle"; no term used outside its defined scope).
   - **Seam 3 — claim ↔ foundations:** traces by valid steps to recognition, not stipulation or circle. *Typed* — definitional / deductive / empirical / testimonial / best-explanation — each with its own standard.
   - **Seam 4 — claim ↔ the web & your own commitments:** no clash with established things, and the standard is applied **evenly** (uniform standards, parity-of-deficiency, no self-exemption).
   - **Seam 5 — claim ↔ its own assertion:** survives self-application (the retortion test).
   - **Seam 6 — claim ↔ the question (argument-mode only):** actually answers the question posed (relevance). Silent when the only question is "is this true?"

7. **Coherence unifies the classic theories.** Because Seams 1 and 3 anchor coherence to *experience*, "correspondence with reality" becomes a *species of coherence* (coherence-with-experience). This is also why my version escapes the standard refutation of coherence-theory (the "coherent fairy tale": a consistent fiction is false because its terms point at nothing recognized [Seam 1] and its chain bottoms out in stipulation [Seam 3]). Even the floor is coherence — between a claim and its own assertion (Seam 5).

8. **Completeness is audited recursively by the floor itself.** The seam-list isn't decreed; a proposed new seam is legitimate only if a clash *there* can make truth indistinguishable from falsehood and doesn't reduce to an existing seam. We stress-tested across all types of false statement (empirical, mathematical, definitional, empty, self-refuting, category error, argument-from-ignorance, double standard, unfalsifiable, hyperskepticism, moral) — every one landed on a seam; none needed a sixth (for truth). "Premises might be false" is not a new seam — a premise is another claim, run the seams on *it* (recursion = my hierarchical logic).

9. **The standing limit (honest, not a flaw).** Seams 3 and 4 are checked against *available* experience, which is open-ended. A claim can pass every seam today and still be false (Newtonian physics in 1850). No method flags a falsehood that has produced no clash with accessible evidence — you can't be refuted by evidence you don't have. So the instrument reports **warrant, not omniscience**; conclusions stay provisional; and when a claim and its rival both pass, the honest output is "underdetermined — here is the open distinguisher," not a manufactured winner.

10. **Expression must cohere with warrant.** The framing layer falls out of the same floor: never sound more distinguished-from-falsehood than your weakest passed seam licenses. Confidence tiers: *firmly known* / *reasonably confident* / *consistent but underdetermined*. No rhetorical bridges; steelman before critique; state limits and support-type up front.

## Corrections made along the way (so they aren't re-litigated)

- The split between Seam 1 (grounding) and Seam 2 (scope) was first called "operational, not deep." **It is deep** — they are coherence across *different relata pairs* (word↔world vs. word↔word).
- The minimal count of checks at the coarsest grain is **three** (determinacy / support / consistency); the working count is **five** (determinacy splits into Seams 1 and 2; consistency into the web and self-application faces), **six** in argument-mode.

## What v1 deliberately is and isn't

- It is the **paste-able** version: floor, mechanism, seams, procedure, confidence/expression, standing limit, self-test, worked-examples table.
- The 86 principles and the DAG live **behind** it as backing, not inline.
- It self-tests (passes its own seams) so it claims no exemption.
