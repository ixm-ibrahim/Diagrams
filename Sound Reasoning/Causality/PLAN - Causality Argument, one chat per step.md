# Plan — Building the Causality Argument, One Chat Per Step

Each step below is sized to a single chat. Each names what that chat takes in
and what it must hand out. The order of the steps **is** the epistemic
structure: nothing is worked before the thing it rests on.

The argument's destination is fixed and bounded: *every outside cause has a
will-with-awareness behind its starter slot; whether those are one will or many
is left open.* The steps are how you reach it without the model running ahead
and back-filling.

---

## The five standing artifacts (the state that flows between chats)

These are not steps. They are the things every chat reads from and writes to.
Keep each as one file. This is your whole tracking system — there is nothing
else to juggle.

1. **METHOD** — the locked rule the whole argument runs on: forward from
   observation; the one ruled-out move is starting from the answer and
   filtering evidence to protect it. Set once, rarely touched.
2. **GLOSSARY** — each load-bearing term: what it points to in recognizable
   things, and its fixed scope. Grows as moves introduce or refine a term.
   Never lets one word carry two meanings.
3. **RATIFIED MOVES** — the closed moves, each written as one clean claim. This
   is the argument as far as it has been settled.
4. **PARKING LOT** — objections raised that are *not* dependencies. One running
   list. Cleared only at the sweep step.
5. **LEDGER** — every move with one of three states: closed / active / open.
   The single page that answers "where am I."

---

## The one containment rule (this is what stops the parroting)

> A derivation chat is fed METHOD, GLOSSARY, and only the ratified moves the
> current move rests on — the ones *below* it. It is **never** shown the moves
> above it, and never told the destination.

With the conclusion in view the model steers toward it and hands you
agreement. Starved of it, it has to test the move on the move's own terms.

---

## Phase A — Lay the foundation

**Step 1 — Lock the method.**
- *Chat job:* state the method as one claim; run it through the audit's
  self-application check (does "reason forward from observation" survive being
  turned on itself?); name what would make the method wrong.
- *Inputs:* the method paragraph from the handoff; the Coherence Audit.
- *Outputs:* METHOD (locked), with its own distinguisher.
- *Done when:* the method passes its own check and you can state what would sink it.

**Step 2 — Seed the glossary and open the ledger.**
- *Chat job:* take each term, write what it points to and its scope, and run
  each through audit Check 1 (points to something recognizable) and Check 2
  (scope holds). Flag any term that points at nothing or shifts scope.
- *Inputs:* the "Words used here" list; the Coherence Audit.
- *Outputs:* GLOSSARY v0; LEDGER with all ten moves marked *open*; empty
  PARKING LOT.
- *Done when:* no term is floating or double-scoped.

---

## Phase B — Derive the spine (one chat per move)

Every Phase B chat does the **same job**. Spelled once here; the only thing
that changes per step is which lower moves it is fed.

> **The derivation-chat job.**
> 1. State the move as one claim in the slot form (CLAIM / TERMS / SUPPORT /
>    DISTINGUISHER / FIT).
> 2. Adversary pass: the model gives the strongest objection it can — *in your
>    defined terms, importing no outside concept.*
> 3. Run the six audit checks on the move.
> 4. Triage each surviving objection: **dependency** (must be settled below this
>    move → it becomes a new open node; go settle it first) or **parked** (→
>    PARKING LOT).
> 5. Rewrite the move to the Writing-Goal register.
>
> *Standing inputs (every Phase B chat):* METHOD, GLOSSARY, the Coherence Audit,
> the Writing Goal, plus the specific lower moves listed below.
> *Standing outputs (every Phase B chat):* the ratified move (or "blocked, needs
> node X"); any GLOSSARY change (locked); PARKING LOT additions; LEDGER updated.

| Step | Move | Fed only these lower moves | What this move lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1 | — (rests on METHOD + GLOSSARY) | cause/doing = an observed correlation; the will is the one thing met directly |
| 4 | 2 | 1 | the refinement: cause = {will, mechanism, effect}, forced by a new observation |
| 5 | 3 | 1, 2 | what is experienced (the command; ease/resistance) and what is not (the somehow) |
| 6 | 4 | GLOSSARY (me-ness) | "outside" defined cleanly — your own body already counts as outside |
| 7 | 5 | 2, 4 | match chains by position; your will lines up with the plant's unknown starter |
| 8 | 6 | 3 | the doing is never observed in any chain, your own included |
| 9 | 7 | 1, 6 | "doing" is part of what "will" means → the will is the one commander you know |
| 10 | 8 | 6, 7 | best-explanation is the only route to the unseen; refusing it proves too much |
| 11 | 9 | 8 | "no-will" is not a free default; observation can't separate it from "will" |
| 12 | 10 | 5, 7, 8, 9 | carry outward in stages: scope (all outside causes), knowing (will-with-awareness), one-vs-many (open) |

Return points (the plan is not strictly straight): in step 4 of the
derivation job, a **dependency** objection inserts a new node *below* the
current move — you drop down and close it before coming back up. A **parked**
objection does not stop you; it waits for the sweep.

## Phase C — Integrate, sweep, express, close

**Step 13 — Whole-argument coherence pass.**
- *Chat job:* run the audit across the **seams between** moves, not inside each
  one. Does move 7 use "will" the way move 1 defined it? Any gap in the chain?
  Any move now redundant?
- *Inputs:* all RATIFIED MOVES; GLOSSARY; METHOD; the Coherence Audit.
- *Outputs:* the integrated argument, or a list of seam-fixes — each fix is a
  new open node, sent back to Phase B.
- *Done when:* every term means one thing across all moves and the chain has no gap.

**Step 14 — Parking-lot sweep.**
- *Chat job:* for each parked objection, check whether the now-closed argument
  actually answers it. This produces your objections-and-answers section.
- *Inputs:* PARKING LOT; the integrated argument.
- *Outputs:* the "questions that come up, and what the argument says" section;
  any objection that turns out unanswered becomes a new open node.
- *Done when:* every parked item is either answered by a named move or reopened.

**Step 15 — Expression pass.**
- *Chat job:* run only the Writing Goal and the audit's exposition layer —
  demystify, pin scope, make connectives explicit, set confidence tiers. No
  logic changes here; this chat only makes the settled argument checkable to a
  reader.
- *Inputs:* the integrated argument; the Writing Goal.
- *Outputs:* the reader-facing version.
- *Done when:* a non-expert can restate each claim, what it rests on, and how
  they'd check it.

**Step 16 — State the bounded conclusion.**
- *Chat job:* write the conclusion and its strength in plain terms — "best
  explanation here," not "certain" — and mark exactly what is left open
  (one-vs-many).
- *Inputs:* ratified move 10.
- *Outputs:* the conclusion, with its strength and its open edge named.
- *Done when:* the claim never sounds more settled than its weakest passed check allows.

---

## Phase D — Plug into the bigger map

**Step 17 — Locate the conclusion in the Truth map.**
- *Chat job:* place this conclusion as a node in the larger argument — which
  parent claim it supports, and which node it now makes the lowest open one.
- *Inputs:* the conclusion; the README / map.
- *Outputs:* the parent claim this feeds; the next lowest open node to work.
- *Done when:* the causality argument is closed and the map shows where to go next.

---

## How to run any single chat (paste-in template)

```
HERE IS MY STANDING CONTEXT:
  METHOD: <paste>
  GLOSSARY (current): <paste>
  COHERENCE AUDIT: <paste or reference>
  WRITING GOAL: <paste or reference>
  LOWER MOVES THIS RESTS ON: <paste only these — no later moves>

THE MOVE TO WORK (active node): <one move>

YOUR JOB, IN THIS ORDER:
  1. State it in the slot form.
  2. Give the strongest objection in my terms only — import nothing.
  3. Run the six checks.
  4. For each surviving objection, tell me: dependency or parked.
  5. Rewrite the move to the Writing Goal.

DO NOT use any concept I haven't grounded. DO NOT reach past this move.
```
