# Sensory Tri-View: consistency audit of the True-Person engine

Rung 2p, 15 November 2026. It covers everything the True-Person view draws inside the glass skull and the timeline numbers that drive it.

Each rule carries one mark.

- **Everywhere**: one rule, applied the same to every thing and every picture.
- **Child rule**: the rule splits by a kind of thing or a kind of picture. Inside each kind it is applied the same.
- **Exception**: one case is treated on its own, for a reason that holds.
- **Offender**: the rule is not applied the same, and no reason holds. Numbered for judgement: O1 to O11 and O13. Each is listed with its picture in Sensory_TriView_Offenders_2026-11-16.html. O12 and O14 turned out to be child rules and are marked so below.
- **Fixed**: an offender this audit found and corrected on 15 November. Numbered F1 to F4.
- **Stand-in**: tied to this stand-in scene. The rung that replaces it is named.

Seconds are the video player's.

**The offenders since your rulings of 16 November.** O1, O2, O3, O4, O5, O6, O8, O9, O11 and O13 are corrected: each is shown before and now in Sensory_TriView_Fixes_2026-11-16.html. O7 is corrected as well, under your word that it follow whichever is more consistent: what his eyes do not see and his memory fills in takes memory's look, and you passed its picture on 16 November. O10 is left for rungs 4 and 5. The lines marked *Offender* below describe the engine as it stood when you ruled.

## 1. What stands in the head

- **The seen picture.** Every place a ray from the simulated mannequin's eyes reaches this frame, drawn as the ray brought it back. *Everywhere.*
- **The last picture.** The picture the eyes held when they shut, standing whole while they stay shut. *Everywhere* for what it holds.
  - It is lit by the eyes being shut alone. A thought lights nothing of it. *Child rule, by your word of the eleventh review.*
- **What completes a picture.** The sky, the ground and the range behind the near things, and the sky behind the range. *Everywhere* outdoors.
  - It exists only through the one window in the east wall. *Stand-in: the room of rung 5.*
- **The known shapes.** Every thing the mind has a shape of, drawn where a thought or a sense lights it and the eyes do not see it. *Everywhere* for the room's things and his own body.
  - The ground, the range and the sky have no known shape (larger than 50 m). *Child rule:* they are completed by the pictures above instead.
  - The window's glass has no known shape and no memory. *Offender O3.*
- **The sun.** A ball of light beyond the sky. The half the rays reach is seen, the rest is memory. *Exception:* it is built apart from every other thing. Its differences are listed under 4. *Offender O5.*
- **The senses.** Glows on the simulated mannequin's skin, sparks, rings, the balance arrow, the organs, the mind's own soul. Listed under 6.

## 2. Where a point is placed

- **Believed distance.** Within 6 m a distance is held as it is. Beyond, it is compressed, keeping the order of things. *Everywhere.*
- **Held whole or spanning.** A thing small against its distance from the eyes (radius under half the distance) is scaled whole and keeps its shape. A thing that spans its distance is placed point by point. *Child rule.*
  - The same thing changes kind as he moves toward it. *Child rule:* it follows from the measure.
  - For the ripple, a thing set into another (a window frame in its wall) counts with the thing it is set into. *Child rule, 15 November.*
- **Flat until resolved.** Until he has looked at the far field for five seconds, everything beyond 6 m lies flat against the sky. *Everywhere.*
  - What counts as "the far field" is decided by the names ridge, ground and sun, not by distance. *Offender O13.*
- **Pressed toward the glass.** What the mind's zoom leaves beyond nine tenths of the glass radius is pressed toward the glass. *Everywhere.*
  - A known shape was placed by its own corner points alone. A wall is one face 4.5 m long, so its middle stayed on a straight line while the seen picture of the same wall followed the curve. *Fixed F1.*
- **The mind's zoom.** The model's scale puts the distance that holds nine tenths of his attention at the glass. *Everywhere* for sight and thoughts.
  - Rays that land on his own body do not count. *Exception:* his body is in front of what he attends.
  - The other senses do not move the zoom. *Child rule:* the zoom follows the eyes.

## 3. How the seen picture is drawn

- **Connected sheets.** Neighbouring rays on one thing are joined into a sheet. Rays on two things are never joined. *Everywhere.*
- **A sheet's edge.** The edge is set where the picture's pixels say the thing ends, half a pixel on. *Everywhere.*
  - Where two things touch, the edge stops at the other thing. *Child rule.*
  - For a thing held whole the edge steps across the eye's ray; for a spanning thing, along its surface. *Child rule.*
- **Faces the eyes see nearly edge-on.** The picture has one row of pixels for such a face. The thing's known shape draws it instead, in the seen look and the picture's colours. *Everywhere* in the seen picture.
  - These tiles sat 2 pixels inside the face and up to half a percent of the distance behind it. They now lie on the face. *Fixed F2.*
  - Whether a face is turned toward the eyes was read off the face as moved into the model. It is now read in the room. *Fixed F3.*
    - A flat-faced thing: by the face itself. A round thing: by its smooth surface, so its rim runs across its facets. *Child rule.*
  - On a thing two or three pixels wide these tiles took the known colour. They now take the thing's own nearest pixels, as the sheet does. *Fixed F4.*
  - On a round thing held whole the picture's own pixels of such a face are left out, so the face is drawn once. On a flat-faced thing they are still drawn in front of the tiles, and a round thing three pixels wide or less is drawn by the picture's ribbon and by its known shape both. *Offender O2.*
- **A round thing's rim.** The last pixels at the rim are left to the known shape. *Child rule* (round things held whole).
- **Colour at an edge.** A pixel beside an edge takes the colour three pixels inside its own thing. *Child rule:* spanning things only. Things held whole take their edge colours from the known shape's tiles.
- **A pixel no sheet takes.** It is drawn as a small square facing the eyes. *Everywhere.*
- **The window glass's reflection.** Drawn as squares only, added as light. *Exception:* glass passes the rays.
- **The picture's grain.** One pixel of the eyes' picture is one point of the sheet. On a face seen at a slant a pixel covers centimetres, so an edge moves by that much as his head moves. *Stand-in:* the 1,024-pixel scan. The piece uses 2,048.

## 4. How a memory is drawn

- **The look.** Colour drained toward grey, dimmer, opaque. A fading memory thins out. *Everywhere* except the sun.
  - **By age.** A memory's colour drains over 2.5 s and its brightness falls to 0.82 over 6 s. *Everywhere* for the last picture and what completes a picture.
  - A known shape is always drawn at the oldest look. *Child rule, by your word of 12 November* (the unseen side of a thing and the memory of a thing are one).
  - What completes this frame's picture is drawn at age nought, close to the seen look. Both it and a thing's far side are places no ray reached this frame, with two looks. *Offender O7.*
- **The ripple.** One ripple for every remembered thing: 3.9 degrees across the eyes' ray, waves 5.9 degrees tall and four times as wide. *Everywhere.*
  - A thing held whole ripples about its own centre, each thing staggered. *Child rule.*
  - His own body's known shape keeps a smaller ripple. *Exception:* his body is felt at every moment.
  - His own body in the last picture takes the full ripple. *Offender O4.*
  - The sun ripples by the same numbers, laid over its own disc. Its light does not rise and fall with the wave as every other memory's does. *Offender O5.*
- **Held still at a seam.** Where a memory meets the seen picture it holds still, and the hold falls away over about eight degrees. *Everywhere* for known shapes and what completes a picture.
  - The last picture has no such hold. *Child rule:* it stands only while the eyes are shut, when no seen picture stands.
- **Giving way to sight.** A remembered place the eyes see again gives way as the seen picture comes to stand. *Everywhere.*
- **Depth beside the seen picture.** What completes a picture stands a hair behind it for the camera. The last picture stands 1.5 percent nearer the eyes. *Child rule.*
- **The lids.** They dim the seen picture and what completes it. *Child rule, by your ninth review.*
  - They do not dim a known shape, the last picture, or the sun's remembered half. *Offender O6* for the known shapes, which also complete a picture.
- **A remembered picture's geometry.** The last picture is drawn by the seen picture's sheets, but not by its rule for faces seen edge-on, nor its rule for a round thing's rim. *Offender O1.*

## 5. Time

- **Shutting.** The eyes must stay shut 0.35 s before the seen picture goes. It fades over 0.3 s and the last picture rises over 0.3 s. *Everywhere.*
- **Opening.** The last picture fades over 0.25 s. What completes a picture switches its picture, its depth, its lid wipe and its age in one frame. *Offender O8.* It shows at the opening after the sun (player 111.3 s): pale patches stand behind every bush and tree for about half a second.
- **A blink** shorter than 0.35 s moves the lids alone. *Everywhere.*
- **Before the first opening** there is no picture. *Exception, by your word of 12 November.*
- **A thought.** It rises over 0.4 s, holds, and fades by its own time on the clock of his awareness. Colour comes 1.5 s after shape. *Everywhere.*
  - The bed's thought rises over 3 s. *Exception, by your word of 14 November.*
- **"Eyes open"** is read at three marks of the lids' cover: under a tenth, under a half, under all. *Child rule:* the three marks answer three questions. Which picture to keep as the last one: lids nearly fully open. Whether the eyes still take in the far view: lids less than half closed. When the eyes count as shut: lids fully closed. (Listed at first as O14.)

## 6. The senses

- **Touch.** A glow on the patch of skin within 1 to 4 cm of another thing, or inside it. *Everywhere* on the sixteen skin parts.
  - A rest level on every part, by rule. *Everywhere.*
- **The spark.** One at each new touch of a pair, read off the contact. *Everywhere* for skin against things and skin against skin.
  - It starts at a joint of the limb, not at the touched patch. For an arm it is the joint nearer the head, for a leg the joint farther from it. *Offender O9.*
  - None from the head. *Exception:* the skull is where the spark runs to.
  - A touch must close to 1.3 cm to send one; the glow begins at 4 cm. *Child rule:* the glow has a soft edge, the spark marks the touch itself.
- **Warmth.** A glow on the skin the sun's rays reach. A rest level on every part, by rule. *Everywhere.*
- **Cold air.** A glow on the skin the window's air reaches, but only on the parts the timeline names. No rest level. *Offender O10.*
- **Pain.** It throbs, 1.6 times a second. *Everywhere* for every pain.
  - Its level leaves out the event's strength, which touch and pleasure use. *Offender O11.*
  - On a part the timeline also touches, it shows on the touched patch and throbs only while it is the strongest of touch, pain and pleasure there. Elsewhere it lights the whole part. *Offender O11.*
  - A sound hurts above a loudness of 0.7 and the sun by its glare, by rule. A hard touch hurts only where the timeline says so. *Offender O11.*
- **The ears and the eyes.** Lit whole, as the heart and the lungs are. No glow on the skin, no waver, no spark. *Child rule:* organs.
- **Awareness.** It dims what the engine derives: rest touch, rest warmth, the glare, the organs, the moving band, balance, sparks from contacts, the rings. It does not dim what the timeline names: touch, pain, pleasure, warmth, cold, the ears' light. *Child rule:* the timeline's own number already says how strongly he feels the thing at that moment. (Listed at first as O12.)
- **The waver.** On the skin's glows. Not on sparks, rings, the arrow or the organs. *Child rule, by your words of 13 November.*
- **Through the limb.** Touch shows through a limb. Warmth and cold do not. *Child rule:* a touch lies between the body and the thing; warmth lies on open skin.
- **Rings.** One every tenth of a second of a sound, a shade of green per source. A source beyond 6 m is drawn at 6 m, not at its believed distance. *Offender O13.*
- **Senses per view.** None in Third-Person, some in First-Person, all in True-Person. *Everywhere.*
