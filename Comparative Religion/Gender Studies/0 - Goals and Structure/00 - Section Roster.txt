SECTION ROSTER

Block-by-block ordering of the framework's sections. Within each
block, sections build on the prior block; reading in this order
means every later section's claims rest on commitments the reader
has already accepted from earlier ones.

================================================================================

BLOCK A — Discourse-level meta
How the conversation about gender works as a conversation. Read first;
the rest of the framework assumes these dynamics.
  01  Discourse Dynamics (DD)
  02  Defensive Patterns (DP)
  03  Attention and Bandwidth (AB)
  04  Online Discourse Ecosystem (OD)
  05  Within-Group Variation (WG)              <- reading instruction

BLOCK B — What can be said, and who hears it
The empirical and reception conditions under which gender claims get
made and received.
  06  What Can Be Said About Biological Difference (BD)
  07  Reception of Observations Like These (PR)
  08  Readership and Coverage (RC)

BLOCK C — Labels, guilt, and language
The vocabulary the discourse runs on, and the moral economy that
vocabulary encodes.
  09  Victim and Oppressor Labels (VO)
  10  Inherited Guilt (IG)
  11  Language and Rhetoric (LR)
  12  Attribution and Character Framing (AC)

BLOCK D — Interpersonal and relational dynamics
How emotional, agentic, and communicative roles are distributed in
mainstream framings.
  13  Emotional Needs (EN)
  14  Agent-Patient Positioning (AP)
  15  Tolerance and Accommodation (TA)
  16  Communication Styles (CS)

BLOCK E — Cultural representation
How gender is depicted and narrated in shared cultural products.
  17  Media and Representation (MR)
  18  Religion, Tradition, and Meaning (RT)
  19  The Predator Framing (PF)

BLOCK F — Sex, romance, and family formation
The mating market, the private sphere, and what happens to children
in it.
  20  Sexual and Romantic Dynamics (SR)
  21  Relational and Private Sphere (RS)
  22  Father Absence and Boys' Outcomes (FA)

BLOCK G — Concrete domains
Where the gender claims meet measurable outcomes: schools, workplaces,
bodies, courts, streets.
  23  Education (ED)
  24  Workplace and Economics (WE)
  25  Health and Mortality (HM)
  26  Reproductive and Bodily Burden (RB)
  27  Aesthetic and Appearance Labor (AL)
  28  Violence and Safety (VS)
  29  Family Law and State Policy (FL)

BLOCK H — Movement over time
How the picture is changing and where it is going.
  30  Political Polarization (PP)
  31  Trajectories Over Time (TS)

================================================================================
READING NOTE

WG (05) is a reading instruction as well as an observation set.
Every "men do X / women do Y" claim in this framework is a
population-average claim with substantial within-sex variance.
The observations are not descriptions of every member of either sex.
