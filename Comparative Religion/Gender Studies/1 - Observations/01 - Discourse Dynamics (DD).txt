--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISCOURSE DYNAMICS (DD)
How argument, evidence, and disagreement behave on loaded topics. Not
gender-specific on its face, but shapes what happens when gender
asymmetries are discussed.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DD1. When a topic is emotionally charged, calm, analytical speech
     passes as enough reason to read the speaker as cold,
     dismissive, or hostile, and often is read that way. The tone
     itself is what the listener judges by — "you sound too
     detached about this" — separate from whether what the speaker
     said was accurate.

DD2. Strong feelings about a position pass as evidence that the
     position is true. "You can see how much this hurts them"
     stands in as an argument, instead of leading to the question
     of whether the claim that's hurting them is true.

DD3. Pointing out that an argument is badly structured (e.g., "this
     mixes two separate issues" or "this premise doesn't support that
     conclusion") reads as disagreement with the argument's
     conclusion — as if the only reason to critique an argument is
     to reject what it's defending.

DD4. A single factual error inside a contrarian argument passes as
     enough to discredit the entire position. The same factual error
     from the mainstream side counts as a local correction; the
     speaker's overall position keeps standing.

DD5. When someone raises an uncomfortable observation, an accepted
     first response is to question the speaker's motives ("why are
     you bringing this up," "who's paying you to say this," "what
     are you really after") rather than to evaluate the observation
     itself. Someone raising an observation that fits the dominant
     view is not standardly asked about their motives first.

DD6. Taboos here are mainly held in place by social punishment —
     mockery, being labeled, lost friends, professional cost, being
     cut out of social circles — rather than by someone producing a
     counter-argument against the claim.

DD7. Speech on these topics is increasingly accepted as itself
     harm or violence. Saying the thing reads as doing something
     to someone, instead of as a claim to be agreed with or
     contested.

DD8. If a listener feels uncomfortable hearing an idea, that discomfort
     is increasingly accepted as evidence the idea itself is harmful,
     rather than as a signal that the listener is running into
     something that challenges a belief they hold.

DD9. "Psychological safety" — originally meaning freedom from being
     personally attacked or humiliated — has come to also mean freedom
     from being exposed to views one disagrees with.

DD10. When women express distress in political debates or public
      forums (tears, visible upset, emotional appeals), it reads as
      testimony that adds weight to their position. When men
      express the same distress in the same settings, it reads as
      instability, overreaction, or evidence they shouldn't be in
      the conversation. You can see the asymmetry in how today's
      media frames the same kind of display by men versus women.
      (This is a claim about how today's mainstream framing tends
      to go, not a universal rule. Specific cases run the other way
      — a woman's distress read as hysteria in some workplace,
      professional, or medical settings; a man's composure read as
      authoritative regardless of substance. The claim is about
      the default reception in today's political and public-forum
      contexts, not every individual case.)

DD11. When a current claim of male disadvantage is raised, an
      accepted response is to bring up historical male advantage
      ("men had power for centuries") as if it disproves the current
      statistic. The current claim and the historical fact cover
      different time periods and measure different things. The mirror
      move — responding to a current female-disadvantage claim by
      bringing up historical female-advantage domains (exemption from
      conscription, maternal-preference custody, dower rights) — is
      not an accepted move and is flagged as deflection when
      attempted.
