--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PREDATOR FRAMING (PF)
The default suspicion attached to men in contexts involving children,
women, or vulnerability.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PF1. Men alone with children in public (a father at a playground
     with his daughter, a man walking a niece home) are routinely
     treated with suspicion regardless of the actual relationship.
     Women alone with children in the same settings are not treated
     with equivalent suspicion.

PF2. Men working in teaching, childcare, pediatric medicine, and
     similar roles face more background scrutiny and more policies
     designed around the possibility they might harm a child than
     women in the same roles face.

PF3. Some airlines have had explicit policies (or informal practices)
     reassigning unaccompanied minors away from seats next to adult
     men. No equivalent policy applies to adult women.

PF4. "All men are potential rapists" circulates as an acceptable
     statement framed as caution. The same kind of statement about
     women — e.g., "all women are potential child abusers" — is
     rejected as bigotry.

PF5. Men crossing the street at night to avoid alarming a solo
     woman is promoted as considerate behavior a decent man should
     do. There is no reciprocal expectation on women to change their
     path to avoid alarming solo men.

PF6. When a man reports being sexually coerced or assaulted by a
     woman, he is frequently disbelieved, mocked, or told he should
     have enjoyed it. When a woman reports being sexually coerced or
     assaulted by a man, she is increasingly supported by default
     and not subjected to that treatment.

PF7. Cases in which an adult woman has sexual contact with an
     underage boy (e.g., a female teacher with a male student) are
     frequently described in media as "affairs" or "relationships."
     Mirror cases — adult man with underage girl — are consistently
     described as abuse, assault, or statutory rape.
