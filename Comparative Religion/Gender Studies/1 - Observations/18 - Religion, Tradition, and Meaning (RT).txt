--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RELIGION, TRADITION, AND MEANING (RT)
What scripts each sex is offered for what it means to live a good life
as a man or a woman.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RT1. Traditional religious frameworks that gave men defined roles
     (provider, protector, spiritual head of the household) have been
     broadly critiqued and largely dismantled in mainstream secular
     culture.

RT2. "Positive femininity" has many mainstream articulations (the
     empowered woman, the nurturing woman, the boss, the wise elder,
     the girl's-girl). "Positive masculinity," when it appears in
     mainstream discourse, is most often defined negatively — as the
     absence of toxic traits ("not toxic," "not fragile") rather than
     as a positive picture of what a good man is.

RT3. Rites of passage that marked the transition from boy to man
     (bar mitzvah, confirmation, tribal initiation, formal
     apprenticeship) have largely disappeared from mainstream secular
     Western culture. There is no broadly agreed-on secular
     replacement.

RT4. Male participation in organized religion has declined faster
     than female participation across most Christian denominations,
     producing congregations that are now majority-female by wide
     margins.

RT5. Secular institutions have produced multiple mainstream
     affirmative visions of what a female life can be organized
     around: career-plus-motherhood, "having it all," the empowered
     professional, the autonomous chooser. They have not produced a
     matching affirmative vision of what a male life can be
     organized around to replace the traditional scripts that were
     dismantled.
