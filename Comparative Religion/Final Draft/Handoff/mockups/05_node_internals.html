Node-card internals — Section 3, Tier 3 sample

Desktop · light
3 · Corroboration

Multiple independent corroborations

Claim
A claim's confidence rises when independent sources — lacking motive or contact to collude — agree on its substance.
So what
Unlocks tier 4 (chain integrity): without corroboration, transmission integrity has nothing to compare against.
Unlocks
  • Multiple corroborations enable chain validation
  • Reduces dependence on any single source
  • Allows scope-appropriate confidence thresholds
Eliminates
  • Single-source claims with no corroboration cannot pass this tier
  • Private revelations not preserved elsewhere
  • Self-attesting claims without independent verification
Unknown remainder
  • Number of corroborators needed scales with claim scope; this tier doesn't fix the threshold
Objections (3)
→Collusion can be undetectablePremise
→"Independent" is unfalsifiable in practiceRule
→Mass delusion caseConclusion
Cost: 7 nodes downstream
