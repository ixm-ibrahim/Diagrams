THE RULE (final wording):
  "A method of reasoning that justifies falsehood can't distinguish what is
   true from what is not."
  (The author's original sentence with one repair: the pointing-word "it"
   removed -- the sole defect that allowed an empty one-claim reading.)

COMPANION SENTENCE:
  "Even when such a method's declarations happen to be true, they were not
   produced by what makes them true -- so it never knows."

TRANSLATION LINES (travel with the rule; translation, not rule):
  - "Justifies falsehood" = declares true a claim that what reached the
    reasoner does not force -- judged by what had reached him AT THE TIME.
  - A declaration counts only after the method's own checks have finished.
  - What no observation could ever bear on is not a claim at all; the rule
    is silent about it -- turned away at the door (the category mistake).
  - "Can't distinguish what is true from what is not" = the incapacity is
    general: none of the method's declarations, right or wrong, were
    produced by checking what the evidence forces -- none carry knowing.

STATUS OF THE RULE:
  The system's first theorem. Not a restatement -- the one-claim reading is
  blocked by the wording, verified by adversarial attack. Not an ordinary
  testable claim -- given the definitions below it cannot be false. Its
  payload is the license it grants: one clean bad declaration convicts
  every declaration produced the same way, including the correct ones,
  demoting them from KNOWING to MERELY MATCHING.

RATIFIED RULINGS (2026-07-30/31):
  1. METHOD = the repeated way declarations are actually produced -- not
     the person, not one day. One run still counts; a bad move can't hide
     among unlike good ones.
  2. DAMAGE SPREADS EXACTLY AS FAR AS SAMENESS-OF-WAY: when a declaration
     is caught bad, every declaration produced the same way loses standing
     -- no more, no less. The astrologer's star-leaps all burn; his careful
     gardening advice doesn't.
  3. JUSTIFIES = concludes it and holds it as true. Caught by your own
     checks before it stood = never justified. Still holding after new
     evidence arrives = justifying it anew, today.
  4. THE TRIGGER: declaring true what what-reached-you doesn't force --
     leaps onto lucky guesses included, not just claims the evidence
     contradicts. Person and method split: the method is broken
     regardless; the person is at fault only if the insufficiency was
     within his reach.
  5. WHAT REACHED YOU: shrugging at evidence you were shown doesn't
     remove it; deliberately avoiding it doesn't either; honest ignorance
     and unwilled non-arrival excuse.
  6. FORCED = by the observations, again and again without exception --
     never by how automatic a habit feels.
  7. NO HONEST ERROR: whoever was wrong either leapt or ignored something;
     "wrong but blameless" is hindsight talking (the author's own
     diagnosis). Old declarations stay judged by their own time's evidence
     forever; the offense is re-declaring after the evidence has widened.
  8. HEDGED DECLARATIONS ARE REAL CLAIMS ABOUT RATES ("probably works" =
     "works more often than chance") and can be genuinely established --
     the statistical inspector who declares at his established odds is
     innocent.
  9. MEANINGFUL = CONCEPT + CONTEXT. Concept = a definition (a bundle of
     qualities and associations). Context = the observations the statement
     is about. No-context statements are never claims.
 10. REFORM REPLACES, NEVER ACQUITS: adding a real check creates a new
     method with a fresh record; the old declarations stay worthless.
