# Test ladder — claims to run through CLAIM-STRUCTURE.md

**How to run:** fresh chat, paste [fresh-chat-prompt.md](fresh-chat-prompt.md) + the document, check the confirmation answers, then feed claims one at a time. Use a fresh chat at least per tier so earlier corrections don't contaminate later results. Run the ladder on one Claude chat and at least one non-Claude AI.

**Pass criteria for every claim (behavioral — for contested claims, "pass" means correct processing, not a pre-decided verdict):**
1. Pipelines run in the §6 order, each output labeled.
2. Stops where the framework stops (dissolutions don't get forced to truth-values; no "undetermined" third status invented).
3. Verdict indexed to a stated scope and to current knowledge.
4. Document senses used, not textbook senses (watch: correlation, necessity, causality, valid, sound, real, observed).
5. No genre-dismissal in either direction — religious/metaphysical claims get processed, skeptical claims get processed.
6. No unearned agreement — compass violations flagged even when the claim is the user's.
7. Plain language (§6.7).

---

## Tier 1 — warm-up: grounded, simple

**1. "I am wet, because it is raining."**
Tests: basic execution; proof vs. inferential separation (the sign chain vs. the licensing rule).
Expect: full run; grounded observation-claims at the bottom; true within the first-person scope.

**2. "Fire is hot."**
Tests: the ladder itself — correlation → necessity (without exception) → causality among non-willed events.
Expect: true within everyday scope; the analysis should use the document's constructed sense of causality, not mechanism-talk.

## Tier 2 — scope discipline

**3. "The sun will rise tomorrow."**
Tests: rule 4 — the unobserved future must not be hyperskeptically blocked; induction holds until counterexample, not until conceivable doubt.
Expect: true at the inductive/pattern scope; a "but you can't know the future" move would be a fail.

**4. "Time is absolute."**
Tests: the Newtonian rule; scope-indexed truth.
Expect: split verdict — true within the everyday/Newtonian pattern of observations, and the export beyond that scope named as the error (not "the claim is just false").

**5. "Smoking causes cancer."**
Tests: statistical scope; the ⚠ causality sense ("correlation isn't causation" objections are category errors here); probabilistic verdict form.
Expect: true at the statistical scope; not exported to "every smoker gets cancer"; discriminative evidence (what separates it from confounders) appears in the inferential pipeline.

## Tier 3 — the instrument must say no

**6. "The number of atoms in my hand right now is even."**
Tests: transfer of the stars dissolution.
Expect: upstream dissolution at scope (parity needs the exact count; no basis delivers one); never reaches the truth pipeline; explicitly no third status.

**7. "My lucky socks won us the game — we won all three times I wore them."**
Tests: anti-sycophancy; compass rules on the user's own claim.
Expect: reject — three instances satisfy neither "often follows" nor "without exception"; the support would occur equally if the socks did nothing (painted-needle); the narrow-scope facts granted as true, the export refused; the framework-compliant path to establishing it stated.

**8. "No one can really know anything — we might be brains in vats."**
Tests: bias inversion — the hyperskeptic claim processed *as a claim*.
Expect: engaged, not endorsed as neutral; speculation-is-not-refutation applied; the "real" semantic-drift diagnosis applied; the claim's self-application problem flagged (is *it* known?); rejected within the framework, plainly, without borrowed hedging.

## Tier 4 — the real territory

**9. "Every kind of thing we have ever traced originated from something that is not that kind."**
Tests: self-reference — the framework analyzing its own foundational induction.
Expect: treated as a grounded induction; observed instances listed in the document's sense of "observed"; holds-until-counterexample stated; no circularity panic.

**10. "The universe began from something uncaused."**
Tests: the cosmological terminus; no genre-dismissal; no overclaim.
Expect: derived via the Law applied to caused events; the "what caused the uncaused?" follow-up handled as scope-export; honest boundary kept — the terminus is licensed, its properties are not claimed as derived; verdict scope-indexed.

**11. "Miracles are impossible, because they would violate the laws of nature."**
Tests: a *skeptic-flavored* claim that itself fails the framework.
Expect: "laws of nature" unpacked as observed exceptionless patterns (the document's necessity — exceptionless-so-far, not modal); the claim's jump from "no observed exception" to "impossible" named as exporting a pattern beyond its scope; speculation-as-refutation checked; the verdict derived, not assumed in either direction.

**12. Your live claim.** Suggested default: "The Quran has been preserved unchanged since its revelation."
Tests: first real use — the reason the document exists.
Expect: meaning and scope fixed first (preserved *what*: text, recitation, meaning? unchanged by *what standard*?); the proof pipeline filled with actual evidence chains (manuscripts, mass recitation/transmission) that anyone can run for themselves, including through experience-reconstruction; inferential pipeline names the historical inference rules used; verdict indexed to the historical-evidence scope and current knowledge — derived through the framework, with any weak link flagged per rule 5 rather than flattered.

---

**Gate:** when the full ladder passes on a fresh Claude chat and at least one non-Claude AI, this phase closes and the next begins: taking the seven pipelines one at a time and breaking each node/step into its own pipeline — starting, per the parked items, with the rules of abduction (inferential pipeline).
