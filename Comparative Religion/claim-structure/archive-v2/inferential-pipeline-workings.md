## Sitting 2 — testing the floor (CLOSED 2026-07-09)

**How it ran.** Twenty-eight challenges: the fifteen inference rules from the project's materials; six of the world's (Occam's razor, Bayesian updating, falsification, inference to the best explanation, expert consensus, mathematical induction); the abduction rules in four families; three free hunts told outright to refute the floor across formal logic/mathematics, statistics/decision theory, and law/medicine/history. Each traced every rival-defense to its bottom and separately steelmanned the best floor-free groundings. Every finding went to an adversarial verifier.

**Result: 27 of 28 trace to the floor; zero counterexamples; free hunts empty.** The world's best floor-free groundings all died on one fork: pressed on *why* their criterion separates good rules from bad (why is "tonk" defective? why does a guaranteed betting loss indict beliefs rather than wallets? why would the simpler theory be *true*?), they invoke truth-discrimination — the floor; kept honestly floor-free, they ground prudence, economy, or procedure, stop claiming truth, and exit the floor's scope rather than refuting it.

**Finding 1 — resolved by the author: the symmetry-breaker is abduction.** The hunt demanded generalization select between "X's are Y" and "X's are Y until t" by itself and called the breaker missing. His ruling: that selection was never induction's job — preferring one induction over another is abduction's job, and the abduction rules that make the selection traced to the floor in the same hunt (the reversal-at-t rival is speculation, nothing observed points at any t; best-fit counts its unobserved reversal-event as an unsupported assumption; an observed reversal is refinement working; "any pattern might reverse" is the hyperskeptic move). Induction's trace bottoms at the floor in two hops, through abduction — the pipeline's own architecture rediscovered from below.

**Finding 2 — resolved by the author: "not performing reason means not aiming at discovering truth."** Concluding "we can't know" is a justification and traces to the floor. The abstainer who concludes nothing is not running a method of reasoning; the aim-at-truth premise lives upstream in the honest-inquirer assumption.

**Lemmas kept:** the two-layer lemma (evidence layers of practical traditions trace to the floor, action layers end in acts not claims and sit outside its scope — the line matches the historical evidence-vs-decision joint in statistics); the tonk probe (every "rules are just conventions" candidate must say why tonk is defective — the covert floor-invocation surfaces there); the bottom's shape (the floor certifies the if-then rule in the same act by which the if-then rule states the floor — mutual presupposition, expected of a genuine terminus, same shape as "phenomena occur" passing itself); the source rule's defense bottoms at the floor in the author's own old words ("falsehoods would become indistinguishable from truth"), remaining gaps are nodes to build; accuracy-dominance is close to a mathematical formalization of the floor.

## Sitting 3 — reducing the floor (CLOSED 2026-07-09, one recording-question open)

**How it ran.** Three independent reductions (one blind — no first cut given; one testing the author's grounding/consistency cut; one hunting for a third component), a judge across them, a completeness sweep forcing all fourteen observation-pile cases onto the components, and a word-licensing audit.

**The reduction, condensed.** A method of reasoning is a shared pattern across sequences of thoughts, each thought following the one before without exception (the click), the sequence ending in — endorsing — a claim. "Justifies falsehood" = ends in a claim deniable given what is currently known. "Independent from truth" = whether the method endorses and whether the endorsed claim is undeniable do not correlate. The anatomy: a start, the dependences between steps, the ending in a claim, and (at the method level) the pattern across processes.

**What converged.** Same anatomy in all three; grouping differed. By location: four places (start, links, ending, class). By kind of failure: two — the author's first cut. Every failure is an absence (something claimed to occur did not — ungrounded start, a click that never happened) or a collision (what is endorsed breaks a grounded, established pattern). The judge: two children; the four places are the map inside them (absence fails at start or links; collision fails at the ending, within/between the claims' terms, or across the method's processes). Completeness sweep: all fourteen cases land; time-is-infinite stays out via the knowledge-index (see the open question below). Notable stretches recorded: the indoors move fires the floor only as the endorsement of a false meta-claim ("the grounded claim is unsupported"); Newtonian export stays inside only because the reasoner possesses the scope of their own observations — dropping something possessed, unlike honest incompleteness.

**Extras that fell out:** the children are ordered (a collision needs a grounded pattern to collide with — grounding first, the order the author already had); the children are genuinely two (circular reasoning: perfect cohesion, zero grounding); the honest-incomplete-reasoner protection may be a theorem of the definitions rather than an attached rule (the open recording-question).

**Verdicts (the author):** (1) "grounded" in the general sense confirmed — "the narrower senses can be its children"; (2) Child 1 accepted (plain: "Ungrounded reasoning cannot distinguish truth from falsehood."); (3) Child 2 accepted (plain: "Inconsistent reasoning cannot distinguish truth from falsehood."); (4) the grounded-node placed upstream on the phenomenological tree (E7), not in the pipeline; (5) bookkeeping approved (narrowing-formula scaffolding; grammar scaffolding; five label additions). The four-locations question was dropped as a question — his "narrower senses can be its children" answers it in principle, and the next layer gets derived at its own sitting, not pre-baked. The honest-reasoner question closed with his ruling: no rule is added at this level — "the learning of new knowledge doesn't mean that the rules of preference are changing, rather we're applying the rules of preference to the new knowledge when integrating it with what we know." The rules stay constant; the inputs grow; the protection is already in the knowledge-indexed definitions. **Sitting 3 fully closed 2026-07-09.**

## The architecture derivation (2026-07-09) — inference, induction, deduction, abduction

**How it ran.** His method, as specified: three seven-pipeline runs (if-then for deduction, generalization for induction, best-fit/selection for abduction), each recursing through every rule its inferential analysis invoked until termination or loop; an architect deriving the relationship map from the traces; an adversary attacking the map with rival architectures and a vicious-twin test.

**What survived (the grounded architecture):** the four names live on different levels, not four peer boxes. Inference is the family (new statements resulting, E4). Deduction and induction are its two speeds split by rival behavior, and the mechanism was found: deduction is degenerate abduction — the rival-space dies on statement, so the click completes the selection alone; induction's rivals survive, so the full selection battery including the want must fire. Abduction is the selection act, two-staged: a want-free comparison (dominance only: never hold a rival strictly worse in coverage-of-what-occurs and speculation-carried — statable without numbers) and a want-involving adoption (withholding always survives statement, so no click compels adoption). The floor is a claim serving as a premise inside ordinary inferences — content, not machinery. Justification arrows: induction cannot run without abduction; abduction's core stands on deduction-shaped steps plus the floor plus occurrence, without induction; in the justification order deduction stands below induction. Two graphs: the justification graph terminates (rules → floor → the want + occurrence; no directed cycle), while the operation graph is genuinely cyclic but benignly — each pass consumes a fresh occurrence (a new click, a new statement-death, a new observed holding): the wound chain exactly as declared. The inference cycle starts at an OCCURRENCE, not a rule (all three runs agree on type, disagree on token).
