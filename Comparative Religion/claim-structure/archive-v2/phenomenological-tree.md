# The phenomenological tree — background tracker

**What this file is.** The tree everything hangs on: each child is a scoped manifestation of its parents, so any node can be traced to its more primitive parents. The tree is many-to-many — a child can have several parents, and a parent several children (the author, 2026-07-09). Background only — it grows as nodes settle and never blocks a sitting. Two moves build it: narrowing (a child is its parent within a narrower context) and reduction (a node's children are found by reducing it to its components — the move that produced the seven pipelines from "claim"). Some rows narrow one parent, others are plain definitions built from several; the Inherits column carries both cases. Licensing is word-level — every word of a node must come from an ancestor's term or Labels/Synonyms set — with three rulings (the author's, 2026-07-09): inflections of a licensed word count as licensed (his A5 "occurrence" on A1's {occur} is the precedent); the narrowing formula "X, within the context of Z" is the tree's construction operator and needs no licensing; and grammar scaffolding — auxiliaries, pronouns inside node wordings, compounds of licensed words, and negation prefixes on licensed roots (un-, non-) — rides on the inflection ruling.

**Sources and authority.** Section A is the author's, settled. Sections B–E are drafts for his correction, assembled from his old projects — `AI/Phenomenology Diagram/3. DAG Definitions.txt` and `Final Draft/Website/data.json` — with CLAIM-STRUCTURE.md winning wherever the old wording differs (its §10 ruling). Rows taken from the old projects keep his wording and label-sets verbatim; rows taken from CLAIM-STRUCTURE.md §3/§5 state its definitions as declarative nodes.

Plain form of the floor (the author's): **"methods that justify falsehood can't distinguish it from truth"** — kept as plain speech, no nodes owed for "can" or "it"; F1+F2 are the grounded form, one rule at two depths. Placement: a scoped manifestation of independence (B17), within the context of a method of reasoning's results against truth and falsehood — provisional; whether a method's results count as B17-phenomena is deferred.

The children are ordered and doubly related (derived and adversarially checked 2026-07-09; the same-day "no parentage edge" note is overturned with cause — record in the workings file). F3 and F4 remain the floor's two children — absence and collision, the two components of its reduction — and F4 is additionally a child of F3 on the joint-act route: endorsing claims together in one act (one process, one chain of dependence, or one answer to one question) claims their co-holding, and a deniable conflict establishes that co-holding absent. **The absence is the crime; the establishable collision is the evidence.** Each child keeps work the other cannot do: F4 convicts where the absence cannot be located (a conclusion known false with no findable failed step convicts today, as a collision), and F3 convicts where nothing collides (the ungrounded start of a perfectly cohesive circle). The paradox stays free on both routes — among true claims the co-holding occurs, undeniably. The lottery author (a thousand grounded claims plus a grounded preface that one must be false) stays free with no special valve: her claims never enter one joint act, and the preface is diagnostic of that, not an excuse; the knowing deliverer of claims whose joint failure he knows is the queued lies descendant. Where only statistical near-certainty of joint failure exists (no specific conflict), the absence is not established and the case stays an honest interim state.

Resolved rulings (2026-07-09, derived from ratified precedents — the enacted-claim ruling, the knowledge-index theorem, the honest-report rule; the author may veto): (1) the joint-claim question: YES, scoped to joint acts, not mere co-endorsement at one index — a statement cannot cancel an enacted claim, so no preface-valve exists or is needed. (2) E7's depth: grounding is graded against experience — a start is grounded when the claim as made occurred (the gauge reading), while endings are judged as said, against what is known (D2); and a step leans on a grounded correlation — it has its resulting even when this instance misses (the miss is the knowledge-index's territory; the crime is a click claimed where nothing grounded connects). The two-gauge case (all readings genuine, endings conflicting) is therefore grounded-yet-inconsistent, completing the test record in the second direction. (3) The two cuts of the floor are both load-bearing, not rivals: the anatomy cut says where absence is claimed (start, steps, joint ending); the collision cut says how it is established. Crossing them derives every settled verdict — absence with establishable collision: convict; absence without: honest miss, no conviction; no absence with collision: paradox, refine; neither: sound.
