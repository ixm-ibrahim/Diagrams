## Method rules — these govern every step of this phase

1. **The construction rule (manifestation form).** Every child in a tree is its parent occurring within a narrower context, framed or reduced to that form. Justification ("A is defended by B") is not parentage. Trees are open: children listed are children found — no closure claimed, no one-to-one forced. *(The author's node methodology, applied to rules in step 2.)*
2. **Rules are claims: meaning before proof.** A rule's concepts must be grounded before its derivation counts — the dependency order (meaning first) applied to the rules themselves. *(The author's catch, step 2.)*
3. **Derivation is not use.** Where a rule comes from (its own claim-structure) and when it acts (its place in another claim's analysis) are different orders. *(Step 1, Part 5.)*
4. **The component rule.** The children of a node come from the components of the node's phenomenological reduction — derived, not brainstormed. This is how "claim" produced the seven pipelines, and how "method of reasoning" produces the abductive primitive's children. *(The author's insight, step 2.)*

These graduate into CLAIM-STRUCTURE.md at step 6.
