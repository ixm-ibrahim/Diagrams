## 0. HIGHEST-IMPACT GAP: plain salt is not in the project

"salt" is the single most-used cooking
ingredient in the dataset. The project currently has only:

 - Salt cod (bacalao) (a fish, not a seasoning)
 - Saltine crackers (a baked good)

No plain salt / kosher salt / sea salt / coarse salt entry exists, and
no "Salt & seasonings" category exists either. Recommendation: add at
least these three ingredients (per-100g values):

 Salt (table) kcal=0 carbs=0 protein=0 sodium=38758
 Salt (kosher) kcal=0 carbs=0 protein=0 sodium=37500
 Salt (sea) kcal=0 carbs=0 protein=0 sodium=38758

Major group: pick a convention. Salt is mineral, not animal/plant/dairy.
Per single-channel rule, the closest fit is `plant` (consistent with how
other inorganic-leaning items like baking soda are categorized). This is
a real edge case worth documenting in CLAUDE.md.

## C. Possible new food group: Beverages

The current 11 food groups don't have a natural home for liquids that
aren't condiments (juice, soda, coffee, tea, alcohol, broth). Options:

 (1) Add a 12th food group "Beverages" and migrate Juices / Alcoholic
 beverages / Soft drinks / Coffee & tea / Prepared soups & broths
 under it. Cleanest semantically.

 (2) Keep 11 groups and slot Juices under Fruits, alcohol under
 Condiments & sauces, broths under Condiments & sauces, coffee
 & tea under Herbs & spices (this is what the current proposed
 taxonomy does). Less semantically pure but avoids touching
 FOOD_GROUPS constant and the validators/UI built around it.

Recommendation: (1) is cleaner but requires updating
food-map-development-plan.md (which lists 11 groups in Phase 13.5),
the filter tree default ordering, and the contains-tag examples.
If aiming for minimal churn, do (2).

## D. Schema: optional 'form' field

Form distinctions (fresh / canned / frozen / dried / cooked / cured)
multiply categories if encoded as separate categories. Example: Fruits
would explode from 47 to ~200 entries if we added every canned/frozen/
dried form.

Recommendation: add an optional `form` field to the schema:
 form: 'fresh' | 'canned' | 'frozen' | 'dried' | 'cured' | 'cooked'
 | 'powdered' | 'paste' | 'pickled' | undefined

Default behavior (when not set): treat as 'fresh' for produce, 'raw'
for meats. This lets recipe-meal authoring match the right form (e.g.
"canned tomato" vs "fresh tomato") without doubling the category tree.

## E. 'contains' tag additions

The recipe dataset surfaces foods that need new `contains` tags or
should pick up existing ones the current ingredients miss:

 - 'alcohol' tag: needs to apply to wine, beer, sherry, brandy,
 vermouth, all liqueurs. Existing only marks Shaoxing wine and
 extracts. When alcoholic beverages are added, ensure the tag.
 - 'caffeine' tag: new tag for coffee, tea, cola, chocolate (cacao).
 Currently no caffeine restriction exists; users with caffeine
 sensitivity (or pregnancy considerations) would benefit.
 - 'soy' tag: many soy-containing ingredients (soy sauce, tempeh,
 edamame) already tag this, but verify miso, hoisin, teriyaki,
 tofu-based mixes do too.

## F. Beverages group implications for restrictions

If alcoholic beverages are added, the existing Halal restriction
(excludes pork, alcohol) becomes meaningful for the first time at
scale. Currently only ~3 ingredients carry the alcohol tag.

## G. group_weights for spanning ingredients

Several ingredients the recipes assume don't cleanly fit the
single-channel group_weights rule:

 - Cream of mushroom soup: dairy + plant (mushroom) - per the
 single-group rule it must pick one. Recommend `dairy`
 (dominant by mass).
 - Egg noodles: grain (plant) + egg (animal) - recommend `plant`
 (grain dominant), tag `contains: ['gluten', 'eggs']`.
 - Velveeta / American cheese: dairy-dominant but contains added
 oil/emulsifiers. Recommend `dairy`.
 - Margarine: plant fat with optional dairy. Recommend `plant`
 (most margarine is plant-based; tag dairy when applicable).
 - Eggnog: dairy + egg. Recommend `dairy`.

Reaffirm the single-group rule in CLAUDE.md or schema.js so the dev
agent doesn't accidentally introduce blended group_weights.

## H. Naming convention for prepared / processed entries

Suggested convention so search and filter UX stay readable:

 Cream of mushroom soup (condensed)
 Chicken broth (low-sodium)
 Bisquick (baking mix)
 American cheese (slices)
 Margarine (stick)
 Whipped topping (frozen)
 Cherry pie filling (canned)

Parenthetical form is consistent with existing entries like
"Tomato sauce (canned)" and "Coconut milk (canned)".

## I. Phase 14 alignment

Phase 14 of food-map-development-plan.md targets ~30-50 categories
with ~80-120 subcategories total. The category gaps surfaced here
would put the count around 51 categories - on the upper end of Phase
14's target. With the new subcategories from MISSING_SUBCATEGORIES.txt,
the total subcategory count grows by ~40-60 new subcategory NAMES (most
within new categories; a smaller number within existing categories).

Phase 14's `tags` array is the right home for cross-cutting labels
like "snack ingredient", "breakfast", form labels (canned/frozen/dried),
and cuisine markers.

## J. Validation step

After adding new ingredients & categories, re-run analyze_recipes.py
to confirm coverage:
 - <5% of recipe ingredient occurrences should remain uncategorized
 - No category should hold >50 ingredients (sign of needing a split)
 - Every food_group should have >=2 categories
 - Every category should have >=2 subcategories (avoid singletons)
